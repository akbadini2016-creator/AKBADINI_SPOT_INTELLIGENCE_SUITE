# FAQ

## Is AKBADINI an automated trading bot?
No. The current production products provide discovery, technical authority and decision-support context. The user controls every trade.

## Does the Scanner require Binance API keys?
No. The current Scanner uses public market data and does not access the user's Binance account.

## Is this for futures?
No. The current commercial generation is Spot-only.

## Why are the TradingView tools not for sale yet?
Their production masters are frozen and ready, but protected paid customer access on TradingView requires the appropriate publishing/access setup. The Pine source will not be distributed publicly as a workaround.

## How many pairs does the Scanner support?
The configured candidate universe contains 261 USDT pairs. The Scanner checks current Binance Spot availability at runtime.

## Are targets guaranteed?
No. Levels and model maps are technical decision-support outputs, not guaranteed future prices.

## Can I get the private source code?
No. The public/customer product does not include private master source repositories.
