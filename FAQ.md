# FAQ

## Is AKBADINI an automated trading bot?
No. AKBADINI provides market discovery, confirmation and risk-review support. The trader controls every decision and trade.

## Does the Desktop Scanner require Binance API keys?
No. It uses public market data and does not access the customer's Binance account.

## Is this for futures?
No. The current commercial generation is Binance USDT Spot focused.

## How many pairs does the Desktop Scanner cover?
The current validated snapshot contains **478 unique classified Binance USDT Spot pairs** with zero cross-category duplication. The snapshot is dated 2026-08-16 and can require revision as Binance listings change.

## What is included in the Desktop edition?
The Desktop edition includes one Master Launcher, six approved Windows category scanners, supporting documentation, validation material and the complete 478-pair TradingView-ready classified text/watchlist lists. It does **not** include Pine indicators.

## What is AI Guidance v2.7.3.1?
AI Guidance is a validated/frozen TradingView decision-support indicator. It evaluates multi-timeframe trend, momentum, relative volume, BTC context, entry quality, preferred retest levels, support/resistance, risk and scenario outlook.

## Is AI Guidance machine learning or an LLM inside TradingView?
No. It uses deterministic decision-fusion logic. It should not be interpreted as a guaranteed predictive AI model.

## What is the First 100 Desktop customer bonus?
The first 100 qualifying paid Desktop customers can receive AI Guidance v2.7.3.1 at no additional cost after their Desktop purchase is verified. Bonus qualification and delivery are handled manually.

## Are targets or signals guaranteed?
No. All levels, scenarios, filters and outputs are decision-support information, not guaranteed future prices or profits.

## Can I get the private production source repositories?
No. Public/customer delivery does not include the private development repositories or internal production history.
